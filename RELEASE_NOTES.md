# 🚀 TrackExpense v1.0.0 - The Financial Revolution is Here! 🎉

We are thrilled to announce the first major release of **TrackExpense**! Your ultimate companion for personal finance management is finally here, polished and packed with powerful features to help you take control of your financial life.

> *"Manage your money with style, track every penny, and achieve your financial goals effortlessly."*

---

## ✨ What's New?

### 🎨 **Stunning UI & UX**
- **Material Design 3**: A modern, sleek interface that looks gorgeous on any device.
- **Dark Mode Support**: 🌙 Easy on the eyes for those late-night budget checks.
- **Smooth Animations**: Enjoy fluid transitions and delightful micro-interactions.

### 💰 **Smart Transaction Management**
- **Easy Tracking**: Log Income and Expenses in seconds.
- **Custom Categories**: Organize your spending your way with vibrant icons and colors.
- **Quick Actions**: "Swipe to Delete" and "Tap to Edit" for rapid management.

### 📊 **Visual Analytics**
- **Interactive Charts**: Visualize your cash flow with beautiful Pie and Bar charts.
- **Deep Insights**: Understand exactly where your money goes with category-wise breakdowns.
- **Monthly Summaries**: Get a clear snapshot of your financial health at a glance.

### 🔐 **Security & Cloud Sync**
- **Secure Login**: Sign in with Google or Email/Password securely via Firebase.
- **Real-Time Sync**: Never lose your data! Everything is synced to the cloud instantly.
- **Offline Mode**: No internet? No problem. Track now, sync later.

### 👨‍💼 **Admin Power**
- **User Management**: Admins can manage users and oversee system activity.
- **Category Requests**: Users can request new categories, and admins can approve/reject them.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
Built with the latest and greatest in Android development:
- **Java** & **MVVM Architecture**
- **Firebase** (Auth, Firestore)
- **Room Database** (Local Caching)
- **MPAndroidChart** (Visualizations)
